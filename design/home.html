How this station works
  1. Every track plays through an official YouTube embed — playback and ad revenue stay with the rights holders.
  2. Set the era, mood, genre and region dials; the rotation reshuffles itself every time you change one.
  3. Dead signals are skipped automatically. The broadcast never stops — just like a real transmitter.
